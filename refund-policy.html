Refund Policy

At Danira Noble Citadel, your satisfaction is our priority. If you are not completely happy with your purchase, we are here to help. Our refund and return policy is designed to be fair, transparent, and easy for all customers.

Eligibility for Returns & Refunds

  • Products may be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery, provided they are in their original condition and packaging.
  • Items must be unused, unwashed, and undamaged. For hygiene reasons, certain products (such as personal care items) may not be eligible for return unless faulty or delivered in error.
  • Please keep your proof of purchase (order confirmation, official receipt, or invoice) for all refund or exchange requests.

How to Request a Refund or Exchange

  1. Contact us within 7 days of receiving your order at support@daniranoble.online or via phone at +63 962 277 0132.
  2. Provide your name, order number, the reason for your request, and any relevant photos if the item is damaged or incorrect.
  3. Our team will review your request and guide you through the next steps, including how to return the item if necessary.

Refund Process

  • Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.
  • Approved refunds will be processed within 3 to 7 business days. The refund will be credited to your original payment method whenever possible, or an alternative option will be discussed with you.
  • Shipping costs for returns are the responsibility of the customer unless the item received was incorrect or defective.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Opened or used personal care products, food items, and other goods marked as “final sale” are not eligible for return unless defective.
  • Customized or special order items may not be returned unless they arrived damaged or with errors on our part.

Order Cancellations

Orders may be cancelled before they are shipped. Please contact us as soon as possible if you wish to cancel or modify your order. Once shipped, our standard return policy applies.
