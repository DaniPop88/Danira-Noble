Lane 3 Race report

From a humble stall to a full shop.

Danira Noble Citadel was established with a vision to provide a one-stop store for the community of Talavera and nearby towns.

Founded by Danica Mae Manantan Espiritu, it grew from a humble stall into a full general merchandise shop serving hundreds of families.

Our mission

To make shopping easier and more affordable by offering a wide variety of merchandise under one roof.

Our values
